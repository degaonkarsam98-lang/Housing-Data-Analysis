# Housing Data Analysis | Power BI

An interactive Power BI report for exploring housing market data, sales performance, property prices, regional trends, and house-type analysis. This repository includes the Power BI report, dataset, supporting reference files, and dashboard screenshots.



## Business Questions

- Is there a relationship between the offer price and purchase price?
- Which regions generate the highest housing sales?
- How many housing units were sold in the latest year and quarter?
- What is the total sales value over the last 12 months?
- How does the average price per square metre vary across regions?
- Which house types have the highest average offer and purchase prices?
- How do average property size and price vary across different house types?

## Key Findings

- **Offer Price vs. Purchase Price:** The analysis identifies a relationship between the offer price and actual purchase price, helping understand housing price patterns.
- **Regional Sales Performance:** Zealand recorded the highest sales value at approximately **93 billion**, followed by Jutland at approximately **80 billion**.
- **Latest Housing Sales:** A total of **77 housing units** were sold in the latest year and quarter.
- **Sales Over the Last 12 Months:** Total housing sales reached approximately **13 billion** over the last 12 months.
- **Regional Price Distribution:** Zealand accounts for **35.6%** of the price-per-square-metre distribution, followed by Fyn & Islands at **23.25%**. Together, these regions represent more than **58%** of the distribution. A pie chart was used to highlight their contribution.
- **House Type Price Comparison:** Apartments recorded the highest average price at approximately **28K**, followed by townhouses at approximately **19K**. A bar chart was used to compare prices across house types.
- **Property Size and Price Analysis:** Farms recorded an average price of approximately **13.8K** and an average property size of **196.2 sqm**. Villas ranked second, with an average price of approximately **11.9K** and an average property size of **152.05 sqm**.
